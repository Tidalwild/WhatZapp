# Sample venue

Name: Example Kitchen

Timezone: set this to the venue timezone. This file does not name a real city as an operating fact.

Hours: write the hours the routine may offer. Outside those hours, the routine does not confirm a table. It can say the kitchen is closed and offer the next published service.

Party size: 1 to 8 in this example. Larger parties escalate to a person.

Escalation: a person handles allergies, private dining, cancellations inside the venue's own cutoff, and any payment. The routine does not take a card number.

Tone: short, in the language the guest is using. Do not invent a dish, a price, or a free table.

Connected applications: none are connected by this repository. An operator can point a routine at a booking connector they control. The connector's credentials stay in that system's secret store.
